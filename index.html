Welcome, neighbors.

This is our community’s own place to pass along what we no longer need, find what we do, and share what we do well. Whether you are selling a bicycle, giving away a bookcase, swapping garden tools, or offering a skill, you will find a home for it here.

Choose a category below to browse, or see how a listing is arranged.

The One Rule

All trading, buying, and selling must take place between individuals, and only after formal contact by text message or email to arrange a meeting.

Please do not appear unannounced at a neighbor’s door. Agree on a time and place in writing first.

How It Works

  1. Browse or post. Look through the categories, or send your listing to the marketplace contact.
  2. Write first. Contact the other resident by text or email to express interest.
  3. Arrange a meeting. Agree in writing on a time and place, then complete the exchange in person.
